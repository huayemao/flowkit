# Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 24, 2026

Welcome to utities.online. We take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

## 1. Information Collection

Most of our tools run on the **client-side (browser)**. This means your raw data (such as images, videos, text, etc.) is typically not uploaded to our servers but is processed locally on your device.

We may collect the following types of information:
- **Access Logs**: Including IP addresses, browser types, referring pages, and access times.
- **Analytics Data**: We use anonymous analytics tools to understand how users interact with our website to improve our services.

## 2. Use of Information

The information we collect is primarily used to:
- Maintain and improve our services.
- Analyze user behavior and optimize user experience.
- Ensure the security and stability of the website.

## 3. Use of Cookies

We use cookies to store your preferences and provide a personalized experience. You can disable cookies through your browser settings, but this may affect the functionality of certain features.

## 4. Data Security

We take reasonable security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access or disclosure. However, transmission over the internet is not 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 5. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services (such as Google Analytics) to help us analyze the use of our services. These third-party services have their own privacy policies.

## 6. Changes to Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
