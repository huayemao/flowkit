# 01 What is Utities?

**Utities (utities.online)** is not just a website; it is the personal product matrix of independent developer **Huayemao**.

It is both a collection of independent works and an ever-expanding product lab. It records the evolution from "solving personal needs" to "serving global users," and carries the technical reflections and creative stories behind the developer.

For Huayemao, Utities is his **digital spiritual home**—a place where code is no longer cold logic, but an art form that simplifies complexity and improves life.

## 02 Design Philosophy: Pure, Private, Boundless

In today's world of information overload and privacy sensitivity, we believe: **some tasks should just be simpler.**

We adhere to the principles of "**Privacy First, Client-Side Only**," dedicated to developing a "Swiss Army Knife" style toolkit that is ready to use, precise, and efficient.

### Core Principles
* **Privacy First (Client-Side Only):** We promote a processing model like Squoosh. From data input and logic processing to result output, everything happens locally in the user's browser. **No registration or login required, and no user files are ever uploaded to the server.** This is not only extreme respect for user privacy but also a conservation of computing resources (green and environmentally friendly, avoiding AI models unless necessary).
* **Seamless Cross-Platform:** Great tools shouldn't be bound by devices. Through responsive design and the flexibility of Web technology, we ensure a smooth, consistent experience on PC, tablet, or mobile.
* **User-Centric Experience:** 
    * **Minimalist Interaction:** Supports file drag-and-drop and direct clipboard pasting to minimize clicks.
    * **PWA Enhanced:** Core tools are built as Progressive Web Apps (PWAs), supporting offline use and system-level sharing, bringing the convenience of native apps to the web.
* **Productivity Leap (Batch Processing):** Say no to mechanical repetition. Whatever can be automated, will be; whatever can be processed in batches, will not be handled individually.

## 04 Developer Whitepaper: Huayemao's Persistence

### Language and Boundaries
As a developer whose native language is Simplified Chinese, Utities includes tools deeply rooted in the Chinese context. However, most apps have been adapted for **internationalization (i18n)**. We hope that through the borderless medium of the Web, users from all over the world can feel the charm of independent development.

### From "Struggling" to "Craftsmanship"
To be honest, the path of independent development is full of challenges. From early "empty talk" to the currently sparse traffic, I have faced lonely backend data countless times.

But the existence of Utities proves the power of **"starting from one's own needs."** Even if only one user saves five minutes because of these tools, this "exceptional skill benefiting others" brand of programmer romance makes it worth writing the next line of code.

**Witness creation here. At Utities, make the complex simple.**
