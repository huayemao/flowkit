Hello everyone, I'm Huaye Mao, an independent developer focused on building minimalist, elegant, and efficient productivity tools.

Today, I'd like to share my latest creation—**[LogoDash](https://logo.utities.online/)**. Its slogan is *"Create beautiful logos in a dash,"*. This tool is designed for independent developers and creators who value efficiency. With no design experience required, you can quickly generate gradient-style icons that adapt to both light and dark modes, helping your product launch faster.


### The Logo Dilemma for Minimalist Developers

As a developer specializing in minimalist productivity tools, I've built five apps in the past month. However, what often delays a product's launch isn't unfinished features—it's the lack of a simple, visually appealing logo.

Since my tools often have both web and desktop versions, a clear, high-quality icon is essential. Yet, I've found myself hesitating to release a product just because the logo "isn't perfect enough." Many independent developers can probably relate to this struggle.

I don't have a professional design background, and creating cohesive, visually appealing logos for multiple apps is challenging. My ideal solution? A tool that lets me customize existing templates, swap out icon elements, and fine-tune the design quickly. That's why I built **Logo Dash**.


### Gradient Backgrounds, Dark Mode Ready

I wanted logos that weren't just visually appealing but also practical. For example, they needed to stand out in dark mode taskbars, where dark icons can blend into the background. A bright or gradient background solves this issue.

Logo Dash currently focuses on gradient background templates, offering a light, modern look while ensuring usability across different scenarios.


### Random Generation, Spark Inspiration

In practice, I often don't even know which icon best represents my product. That's why, in addition to manual icon selection and color adjustments, I added a *"Random Generate"* feature.

You don't need a clear direction from the start. Just click to generate random options, and inspiration might strike. This is the kind of effortless experience I wanted Logo Dash to provide—no pressure, just play around, and ideas will come naturally.


### If You Need a Hassle-Free Logo

If you're preparing to launch a project and need a quick logo—without learning design from scratch or dealing with the unpredictability and cost of AI tools—give Logo Dash a try.

It may not deliver a bespoke, high-end design, but it will help you create a clean, functional, and versatile logo in the shortest time possible. For new products, *"launch now"* is often more important than *"launch perfect."*

Whether it's for a website, mini-program, or app, a logo that doesn't hold you back can help you take that final step toward launch faster.
